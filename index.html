THE POSNO CHARACTER

A florist remembered for craft, occasion, and community.

Posno Flowers has a story most florist templates cannot manufacture: a genuine St. Petersburg history, founders with a performing-arts life, and decades of place-based memory.

The visual system borrows from floral composition and classic editorial design—warm paper tones, deep botanical green, aubergine ink, dramatic serif type, and photography that lets the flowers stay central.

COMPOSITION, NOT DECORATION

A visual rhythm inspired by the founders' musical life.

OUR STORY

A St. Petersburg name with roots going back to 1961.

William and Rosaline “Rose” Posno built Posno Flowers in 1961. Rose's obituary later described it as one of the city's largest flower shops and a local fixture for more than 25 years.

Florida records show that POSNO FLOWERS, INC. was incorporated on May 31, 1978 and later administratively dissolved on September 25, 2009.
